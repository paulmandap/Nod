How it works

Three things, on a loop.

It hears

Whatever your speakers play, turned into text on your laptop.

It reads

The slide being shared, including the small print.

It whispers

One short line at the top of your screen.

Every second, forever, until you close the laptop.

The placement

Why the top
of the screen?

Because that's where your camera is.

You read the line without ever looking away from the people you're talking to. Glance down at a second monitor and everyone on the call can tell.

The design rule

You get one second.
So we designed for one second.

1

One strong line

The fact itself, never a description of it. “Launch moved to November” — not “They discussed timing.”

2

A bar that drains

It tells you whether what you're reading is still true, without costing you a word of reading.

3

Almost no movement

Animation at the top of the screen pulls your eyes off the camera. That's the exact thing we're preventing.

Honest numbers

The part we care about most

Your meeting stays yours.

Nothing is recorded

No audio file is ever written. There is nothing to leak.

The listening happens here

Whisper runs on your machine, not in someone's data centre.

Two minutes of memory

Anything older is simply gone. Not archived — gone.

Recording a call has real rules in most places. The best version of this tool is one you can announce at the start of the meeting — so we built that version.
